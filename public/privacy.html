Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

STKD ("we", "us") is a website for rating films, series, games, books, manga, music and podcasts and sharing them with friends. It is run by Efe Özcan in the Netherlands. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have under the GDPR. Questions? Email hello@stkdapp.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Profile: username, display name, bio, avatar emoji and colours, and your "top 6".
  • What you post: ratings, comments, statuses, hot takes and agree/disagree votes.
  • Social data: friend requests and friendships, people you block, and reports you file.
  • AI assistant: a daily count of how many AI requests you made (to enforce limits). We do not store the content of your AI chats.
  • Technical data: your login session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Our hosting providers process IP addresses and basic request logs to run and secure the service.

We don't use advertising, tracking cookies or third-party analytics, and we never sell your data.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • To provide STKD to you: your account, profile, ratings and social features (performance of a contract).
  • To keep STKD safe: rate limits, content filtering, reports and blocking (legitimate interest).
  • To answer you when you contact us (legitimate interest).

Who can see what

  • Your username, display name, bio and avatar are visible to all logged-in users, so people can find and add you.
  • Your hot takes and votes are visible to all logged-in users.
  • Your ratings and comments are visible only to you and your accepted friends.
  • Reports are visible only to us.

Services we use

We share data only with the providers needed to run STKD:

  • Supabase: database and login (servers in London, UK).
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Anthropic: powers the AI assistant. The messages you send it are processed to generate a reply.
  • Email provider: sends account emails such as sign-up confirmation.
  • Media databases: when you search, your search text is sent to TMDB and RAWG (via our server) and to Open Library, Jikan (MyAnimeList) and Apple iTunes (directly from your browser, so they can see your IP address).

Some of these providers are located outside the EU/EEA (for example in the US or UK). Where that happens, transfers are covered by an adequacy decision or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, ratings, takes, votes, friendships and other data are deleted immediately from our database. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict how we use it. You can edit your profile and delete your account yourself (My Profile → Edit → Delete my account). For anything else, email hello@stkdapp.com and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Age

STKD is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
